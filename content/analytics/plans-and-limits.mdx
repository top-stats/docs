TopStats does not sell fixed "plans" with feature checklists. Pricing is
**plan-less and usage-based**: you choose how many events you expect to send each
month and how long you want to keep your data (retention), and the price scales
continuously with both. There is no "Pro" tier that unlocks funnels or a
"Business" tier that unlocks alerts. There are only two states, free and paid,
and every paid workspace gets the same thing: every feature and unlimited team
members, whatever you spend.

<Callout title="Two knobs, one price">
  The only things that move your price are **monthly event volume** and
  **retention**. More events or a longer retention window cost more; fewer events
  or a shorter window cost less. Spending more never unlocks extra features, it
  only buys headroom.
</Callout>

## Free vs paid

| | Free | Paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly events | 2,000,000 (hard cap) | Usage-based, metered |
| Data retention | 30 days | 30 days up to 2 years |
| Team members | 1 | Unlimited |
| Environments | 2 | Unlimited |
| Features | Everything except the five below | All |

The free tier is a real, complete workspace, not a trial. Every widget type,
every chart, alerts, digests, annotations, external sources, status pages, stat
badges, and sharing and embedding all work on free exactly as they do on paid.
Five features are the exception.

## What needs a paid workspace

These five features require a paid workspace:

- [Saved segments](/docs/analytics/segments)
- [Groups](/docs/analytics/groups)
- [Issues](/docs/analytics/issues)
- [Feature flags](/docs/analytics/flags)
- [Data labels](/docs/analytics/data-labels)

On a free workspace they are hidden from the navigation, and calling their
endpoints directly returns a `402` with the message
`Upgrade to a paid plan to use this feature`.

<Callout title="This is the only feature difference">
  Free and paid differ in exactly two ways: the volume limits in the table
  above, and these five features. There is nothing else a paid workspace can do
  that a free one cannot.
</Callout>

## The free monthly cap

The free tier includes **2,000,000 events per month**, and that number is a
**hard cap**. Once you reach it, ingest stops accepting new events: a
`POST https://analytics.topstats.gg/v1/events` call returns
[`402`](/analytics/limits-and-errors) until the next monthly cycle, or until you
upgrade. Nothing you already sent is lost; you just cannot add more that month.

<Callout type="warn" title="Free is a wall, paid is a meter">
  On the free tier, hitting the cap **rejects** further events (you get a `402`).
  On a paid workspace, event volume is **metered**, not rejected: events keep
  flowing past your chosen level and the extra usage is simply counted toward
  what you pay. Paid workspaces never lose data to a cap at ingest time.
</Callout>

For example, if you run a game that emits a `player_join` event every time
someone logs in and a couple of `match_end` events per session, a busy weekend
could push you past two million events quickly. On free, the events after the cap
would be turned away with a `402`; on paid, they would keep landing and show up
in your dashboards, and your usage for the month would tick up.

## Data retention

Retention is how long TopStats keeps your events available to query. It is not the
same thing as the [Retention widget](/analytics/types/retention), which measures
whether people come back. Here, retention just means: how far back in time your
dashboards, the [Events stream](/analytics/events-stream), and
[Actors](/analytics/actors) can look.

The free tier keeps **30 days** of data. Paid workspaces let you pick a longer
window, anywhere from **30 days up to 2 years**. A longer window is useful when
you want to compare this quarter to last quarter, chart a full year of growth, or
keep a compliance trail; a shorter window keeps costs down when you only ever
look at recent activity.

### Retention presets

When you set retention on a paid workspace, you pick from these presets:

| Preset | Length |
| --- | --- |
| 30 days | 1 month |
| 3 months | ~90 days |
| 6 months | ~180 days |
| 1 year | 365 days |
| 2 years | 730 days |

Choosing a longer preset raises your price; choosing a shorter one lowers it,
along with your monthly event volume being the other factor.

## The white label add-on

[White label](/docs/analytics/white-label) is the one thing you buy separately
rather than by turning a knob. It is a flat **10 EUR per month** on top of
whatever your usage costs, and it does not change with event volume or
retention.

It is not restricted to paid workspaces: a free workspace can buy the add-on on
its own. Only a workspace **Owner** with a verified email address can add or
remove it.
